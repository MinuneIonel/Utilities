# What's new in 0.4.1

Fixes for 0.4.0:

- Dither sliders drag again. In 0.4.0 you could only click a spot on them.
- Scrolling over a slider never scrolls the settings panel anymore, even when you change direction mid-scroll.
- ⌘A, ⌘C and ⌘V (Ctrl on Windows and Linux) in a number box work on the number again, instead of selecting or duplicating nodes.
- A saved Dither preset stays selected when you change a setting and put it back.
