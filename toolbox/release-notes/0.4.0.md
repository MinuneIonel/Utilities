# What's new in 0.4.0

**Remove background**
- A new node that cuts the subject out of a photo: keep it on transparency, put it on a colour, or wire in a new background. It can also output just the mask, and has settings for edge hardness, edge refinement, growing or shrinking the cut-out, and inverting it. It works on many photos at once and on video too.
- Two models: *Fast* and *Best*. Each one downloads once the first time you use it, then works offline.
- New workflows: *Remove the background* from one photo or many.

**QR codes**
- A new node that makes a QR code for a link, text, Wi-Fi login, email, phone number or SMS. Save it as a PNG or an SVG, or run it through Dither first. Start from the *QR code for a link* workflow.

**Presets and undo**
- Save the settings of any node as a named preset and pick it again later, for Dither recipes and everything else. Presets stay after a restart.
- Undo and redo now have buttons in the top bar, and ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z undoes slider and menu changes too.

**Scroll to nudge sliders**
- Hover a slider or number box and scroll to nudge it, like in Blender. Hold Shift for one step at a time, or ⌘/Ctrl for bigger jumps. Scrolling the panel still scrolls the panel.

**Transparency**
- GIFs and WebM videos now keep transparent areas. Formats that can't (MP4, JPEG and most ProRes) turn them black instead of showing leftover pixels. Previews show transparency over a checkerboard.
