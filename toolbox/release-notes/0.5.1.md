# What's new in 0.5.1

**Simple view**
- New to Toolbox? It now opens on **Simple view**: pick what you want to do (download a video, compress photos, make a PDF, a QR code…), give it your file or link, and press one button. Files go to your Downloads folder, or a folder you pick.
- **Open as graph** shows the same job as a node graph, and the **Simple | Graph** switch in the top bar goes back and forth. If you already have a graph, Toolbox keeps opening in Graph view.

**Play before rendering**
- The big preview behind the graph has a **Play** button (or press Space): watch a video with its effects and hear its sound before you render. Change a setting while it plays and the picture follows.
- If your computer can't keep up, it plays slower and says so. Sound files get their own Play button.

**Tabs**
- Each graph opens in its own tab, with its own undo. Picking a workflow opens it in a new tab.
- **Save graph** (⌘S) saves over the graph's file without asking; **Save graph as…** always asks. **File > Open Recent** lists your last graphs, and double-clicking a .toolbox file opens it in Toolbox.
- Every tab comes back when you reopen Toolbox, unsaved changes too.

**Long videos**
- Long and 4K videos no longer run out of memory: results are kept on disk while you work and copied when saved.
- One video feeding two outputs (say an MP4 and a GIF) is read once, so rendering both takes about as long as one.

**Fixes**
- Opening a graph right after launch could put a file from your last session into it. Fixed.
