# What's new in 0.5.2

**Timeline**
- Videos and sounds now get a timeline under the graph: a strip of pictures (or the sound's wave) you can drag on. Trim a clip by dragging its ends, double-click to place where Split cuts, drag across to pick parts, or mark the bit Cut audio takes out.
- Time boxes take times the way you'd write them: 1:05.5, 65.5 or 1:02:03. The old two-hour limits are gone.

**Folder of files**
- A new input, **Folder of files**, runs the same job on every photo in a folder (and its sub-folders, if you like). Save keeps the sub-folders.
- Turn on **Only new files** and next time it only does the photos added since: handy for the same job every week. **Forget what was done** starts over.
- Two new workflows: **Compress a folder of photos** and **Watermark a folder of photos**, also in Simple view.
- Like with files, a graph from someone else that names a folder on your computer asks before reading it.

**Run a graph without the window**
- `Toolbox --run my-graph.toolbox --out ~/Pictures/out` runs a saved graph with no window and exits, so it can run on a schedule or from Shortcuts. `--input` gives it files or a folder, `--set` changes a setting for that run. The README has the details and examples.
