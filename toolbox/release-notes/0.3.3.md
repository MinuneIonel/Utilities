# What's new in 0.3.3

**Cut audio**
- A new node that keeps just one part of a sound, or takes a part out and joins what's before and after it. The file keeps its own format unless you pick another.

**Split into clips**
- Cuts one video or sound into several shorter files: every so many seconds, into equal parts, at the times you list (`0:30, 1:15`), or just the parts you list (`0:10-0:25, 1:00-end`). Wire it into Save and you get one file per clip. It also works after effects like Dither.
- Two new workflows to start from: *Split a video into clips* and *Cut a part out of a sound*.
